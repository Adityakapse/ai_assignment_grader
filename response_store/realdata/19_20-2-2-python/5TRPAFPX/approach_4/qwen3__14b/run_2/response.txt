### **Understanding the Problem Context**

In RPN, operators act on the two most recent numbers on the stack. For example, the expression `3 4 -` evaluates to `3 - 4 = -1`. The stack processing order is such that:

- When the operator is encountered, the **first popped number** is the **right operand**, and the **second popped number** is the **left operand**.
- So, the operator is applied as: **`left_operand` operator `right_operand`**.
